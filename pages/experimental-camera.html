Experimental Camera

an experimental camera using ml5.js models (FaceMesh and BodyPoze-BlazePoze) to make a commentary on the objectification of women in society. meant to be used by men, or people who participate in this objectification, it crudely assigns negative and positive points to facial features, points out parts of the female body that are most sexualized, and gives a "sex appeal" score to the user. everything is entirely randomized, as the point is not to judge the user, but to judge this facet of society; an ongoing struggle that women face.
